Thailand Climate Extremes (ERA5-EX)

Annual climate-extreme indices on a 0.1° grid over Thailand, 1950–2025, computed from ERA5-Land daily temperature and precipitation. Each cell shows one year; scrub the year slider or press play to animate. Anomaly mode shows the difference from the 1991–2020 mean.

Percentile thresholds use the 1961–1990 baseline. Ocean and no-data cells are blank.

Temperature extremes

CSDI — Coldwave days (days)
Days in cold snaps — 6+ consecutive days of unusual cold.
DTR — Day-night swing (°C)
Average difference between afternoon high and night low. Shrinks when nights warm faster than days.
FD — Frost nights (days)
Nights below 0°C. Rare in Thailand outside high mountains.
ID — Ice days (days)
Days that never get above 0°C. Virtually absent in Thailand.
SU — Summer days (days)
Days hotter than 25°C. Counts how long summer-like heat lasts each year.
TN10p — Cold nights (%)
Share of nights colder than 90% of nights in 1961–1990.
TN90p — Warm nights (%)
Share of nights warmer than 90% of nights in 1961–1990.
TNn — Coldest night (°C)
The coldest nighttime low of the year.
TNx — Warmest night (°C)
The warmest nighttime low of the year. High TNx means nights don't cool down — the dangerous part of heatwaves.
TR — Tropical nights (days)
Nights warmer than 20°C — nights with no relief from heat.
TX10p — Cool days (%)
Share of days with unusually cool afternoons (cooler than 90% of days in 1961–1990). Shrinking as the climate warms.
TX90p — Hot days (%)
Share of days with unusually hot afternoons (hotter than 90% of days in 1961–1990).
TXn — Coldest day (°C)
The coldest afternoon temperature of the year. Rising TXn means winters are losing their coldest days.
TXx — Hottest day (°C)
The hottest afternoon temperature of the year. Rising TXx means more intense heat peaks.
WSDI — Heatwave days (days)
Days in heatwaves — 6+ consecutive days of unusual heat.

Rainfall extremes

CDD — Longest dry spell (days)
Most consecutive days with under 1mm rain — the drought number.
CWD — Longest wet spell (days)
Most consecutive days with 1mm+ rain.
PRCPTOT — Yearly rainfall (mm)
Total rain for the whole year.
R10mm — Heavy rain days (days)
Days with 10mm or more — solid downpours.
R1mm — Rainy days (days)
Days with at least 1mm of rain.
R20mm — Very heavy rain days (days)
Days with 20mm or more — the kind that floods streets.
R95p — Very wet days total (mm)
Total rain falling on the wettest 5% of days. Grows when extremes intensify.
R99p — Extremely wet days total (mm)
Total rain on the wettest 1% of days — the rarest deluges.
RX1day — Heaviest downpour (mm)
The most rain falling in a single day each year — the flash-flood number.
RX5day — Heaviest 5-day rain (mm)
The most rain in any 5 consecutive days — the river-flood number.
SDII — Rain intensity (mm/day)
Average rain per rainy day. Rises when rain comes in harder bursts.

Compound events

cold_day_night — Cold days + cold nights (days)
Days where both afternoon and night were unusually cold.
cold_heavy_rain — Cold + extreme rain (days)
Unusually cold days with extreme rainfall.
dry_cold — Cold + dry days (days)
Days both unusually cold and dry.
dry_hot — Hot + dry days (days)
Days both extremely hot and dry — drought-heat combos that stress crops and power grids.
warm_day_night — Hot days + warm nights (days)
Days where both afternoon and night were unusually hot — no overnight relief.
warm_heavy_rain — Hot + extreme rain (days)
Extremely hot days with extreme rainfall — the worst flash-flood setup.
wet_cold — Cold + rainy days (days)
Days both unusually cold and rainy.
wet_hot — Hot + rainy days (days)
Days both extremely hot and rainy — tropical downpours in heat.
