About this data

Groundsource: A Global Dataset of Flood Events from News. 2,646,302 flood events worldwide extracted from 5M+ news articles (80 languages) using Gemini LLMs, geocoded via Google Maps. This site shows the 70,795 events in Thailand (2000-11-20 to 2026-01-26). The source extract used a rectangular bounding box, so 28,402 events falling entirely outside Thailand's borders (neighboring countries and sea) were removed; border-crossing events are kept whole.

What each event is

One row = one spatiotemporal flood observation: a polygon (administrative area or buffered point) with start/end dates and area in km². The dataset is entity-based, not meteorology-based — one large flood can appear as many entries for different reported places.

Known biases

  • Recency bias: ~64% of events are 2020–2025 (more digitized news, not more floods).
  • Media bias: coverage follows digital news infrastructure; recall is lower where local news is sparse.
  • Severity bias: high-impact events are over-represented vs. minor ones.
  • Geocoding errors may cluster by region/language.
